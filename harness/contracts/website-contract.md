# Website Contract

The Quartz website is the public reading surface.

## Public Rule

Only finished, stable, written, or publishable artifacts appear publicly.

The site must not display plans, scaffolds, raw notes, or AI-generated structures as if they were finished work.

## Public Center

Proustian / Recherche remains the public center.

Language Training and Writing are core capability lines.

Constellations replaces Maps.

## Backstage Areas

System and 99_Archive are backstage.

They may exist for operations, preservation, and recovery, but they must not be promoted as public pillars.

## Prohibited Website Drift

- No generic blog posture.
- No LMS or SaaS posture.
- No future app promises.
- No public textbook chapters before Harness review.
- No raw HTML in Markdown pages unless separately approved.
