# Project Memory

Proustian is a long-term reading, language, writing, and self-formation project centered on *In Search of Lost Time*.

Recherche / Proust is the mother star. Other fields, theories, images, mathematics, AI, and concepts may orbit or transfer through it, but they must not replace it.

The project is not expanding now. The current core is language training and writing thickening. Structure should clarify boundaries and preserve work, not advertise future completeness.

## Public Surface

The public site shows only finished, stable, written, or publishable artifacts.

Public pages must not make the project look more complete than it is.

System and 99_Archive are backstage. They may preserve operational or retired material, but they must not become public pillars.

Maps is replaced by Constellations. Constellations means a restrained field of good neighbours, motifs, transfer relations, and edge-to-core resonances that return to Recherche.

## Language Layer

Language Training is the precision and recomposition layer of Proustian.

It has three tracks:

- French Precision
- EN-CN Recomposition Studio
- Comparative Literary Induction

Comparative Literary Induction is the third textbook line of the language layer. It treats Proust in English translation as part of the English literary world and uses comparative literary exercises, close reading questions, and critical writing prompts to train judgment.

It may learn from Norton-style literary pedagogy: brief context, text anchor, comparative author or text anchor, close reading questions, comparison drills, critical debate prompts, short writing assignments, AI feedback after the learner attempt, and a final concept / comparison card.

It is backstage / working by default until selected units become finished public artifacts. It may inform EN-CN recomposition, French precision, and Proustian reading, but it must not become a public maze or uncontrolled expansion layer.

Textbook and translation materials require Harness before publication.

Human-first language pedagogy is now a stable rule. Learner attempt precedes AI feedback. Basic explanation remains, but it must serve practice.

The default chapter loop is:

Core Explanation -> Input -> Try -> AI Feedback -> Repair / Retry if needed -> Card -> Reuse

The denser EN-CN training loop is:

Core Explanation -> Input -> Recognition Drill -> Recomposition Drill -> AI Feedback -> Repair Drill -> Thinking Question -> Transfer Drill -> Teacher Note -> Movement Card

Every EN-CN chapter must include a primary work zone, a concrete marking target, a rough Chinese output, one local repair, one transfer task, and one judgment question.

The durable direction is: explain lightly, train heavily, feedback precisely, record minimally.

## File Over App

Applications are temporary interfaces. Files are durable assets.

The durable project asset is the file system: Markdown, state boards, decisions, contracts, and reviewed content.

## AI Boundary

AI may assist with structure, checks, state preservation, and bounded patching.

AI must not replace core reading, judgment, writing, or the user's intellectual authorship.

In language and textbook work, AI may provide materials, examples, sound, feedback, correction, and card compression. AI must not provide a final translation, full sentence skeleton, movement map, full interpretation, or finished answer before the learner has genuinely attempted the task.
