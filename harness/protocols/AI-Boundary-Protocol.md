# AI Boundary Protocol

AI is a post-check and structural assistant.

AI may support:

- state restoration
- link repair
- structural cleanup
- verification
- restrained scaffolding
- review checklists

AI must not replace:

- primary reading
- reader attempts
- judgment
- writing formation
- intellectual authorship

## Language Materials

For EN-CN work, do not produce a final translation unless the reader has first attempted the passage or explicitly requests a final rendering.

For textbook work, do not generate public chapters before Harness review.
