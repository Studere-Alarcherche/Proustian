Rules added:

1. Do not paste long source passages into chapters.
2. Do not invent source text.
3. Use short anchors only after passage boundary verification.
4. A passage should not move to `selected` until source, version, location, start words, end words, and short citation anchors are confirmed.
5. Each annotation must support a training action, not decorative commentary.

Common Errors to Watch:

1. Using quotations as literary decoration instead of evidence.
2. Pasting too much source text into the chapter.
3. Annotating theme while ignoring syntax.
4. Treating a short anchor as a substitute for full passage reading.
5. Moving from citation directly to Chinese meaning without grammar and movement notes.

Skills to Update:

- Sentence Movement Analyst: require short source anchors for every skeleton claim.
- EN Style Reader: tie rhythm and breath claims to quoted anchors.
- CN Recomposer: justify recomposition choices through movement notes.
- Exercise Builder: include citation-annotation tasks in each practice mode.
- Pattern Distiller: require citation evidence before promoting a pattern.

Next Cycle Warning:

Chapter 5 and Chapter 6 should use the same citation layer but should not become quotation notebooks. The anchor must serve slow perception and review, not replace the chapter move.
