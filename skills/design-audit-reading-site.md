# Design Audit Reading Site

Human-readable mirror for the `design-audit-reading-site` repo-local skill.

Use this when reviewing a Proustian Quartz or app surface for reading support.

## Audit Questions

- Does the page support sustained reading?
- Is Proust visually and conceptually central?
- Are hierarchy, spacing, typography, and contrast calm enough for close reading?
- Does navigation help the reader without creating dashboard pressure?
- Do Language Training, System, and Daily remain service layers?
- Does the page feel like a literary observatory, not a blog, LMS, SaaS, or admin dashboard?

## Preferred Mood

- dark archive
- observatory
- reading chamber
- manuscript texture
- memory palace
- textual apparatus

## Avoid

- SaaS style
- generic card grid
- bright cheap yellow
- marketing CTA
- LMS aesthetics
- crowded dashboards

## Output

- Surface audited
- What supports reading
- Risks
- Required changes
- What not to change
