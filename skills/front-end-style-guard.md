# Front End Style Guard

Proustian frontend style guard for Quartz and future app surfaces.

## Design North

The site should feel like:

- textual observatory
- reading chamber
- dark archive
- archive garden
- textual apparatus

Prefer dark archive, quiet gold, spacious reading surfaces, slow emergence, restrained navigation, and typographic hierarchy.

## Avoid

- SaaS landing page aesthetics
- LMS or classroom interface mood
- admin dashboards
- generic AI cards
- bright cheap yellow
- oversized pill buttons
- muddy brown-gold parchment
- decorative systems that compete with reading

## Checks

- Proust remains visually and conceptually central.
- Typography, spacing, and contrast support sustained reading.
- Language Training, System, and Daily behave as service layers, not the center.
- The page feels like a literary observatory rather than a blog, LMS, SaaS, or admin dashboard.
