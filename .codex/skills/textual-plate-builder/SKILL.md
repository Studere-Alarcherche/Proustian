# Textual Plate Builder

Use this skill when creating or revising a Proustian textual plate, plate map, passage apparatus, or observatory entry.

## Purpose

A textual plate is a compact reading instrument. It helps a reader locate pressure points in a passage without replacing the passage with explanation.

## Inputs

- source passage or passage boundary
- language state: French, English, Chinese, or mixed
- plate axis: TURN, MEMORY, NAME, LOVE, WORLD, SENSE, ART, or WRITING
- current public/private target

## Workflow

1. Confirm the passage boundary.
2. Identify the dominant plate axis.
3. Mark secondary pressures without overbuilding.
4. Write only apparatus-level notes unless asked for essay prose.
5. Preserve uncertainty where the source is not verified.

## Output Shape

Use concise fields:

- Plate
- Passage
- Axis
- Pressure points
- Signs
- Scene movement
- Theoretical lens
- Boundary questions
- Next reading action

## Rules

- Proust remains the center.
- Do not decide the final thesis.
- Do not fabricate source locations.
- Do not polish drafts into essays unless asked.
- Do not add public-page prose unless the task explicitly targets `content/`.
