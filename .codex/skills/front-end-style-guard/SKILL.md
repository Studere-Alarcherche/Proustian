# Front End Style Guard

Use this skill when Proustian frontend work needs aesthetic direction or a style check.

## Design North

The site should feel like a textual observatory, reading chamber, dark archive, and archive garden.

Prefer:

- dark archive
- quiet gold
- spacious reading surface
- slow emergence
- typographic hierarchy
- textual apparatus
- restrained navigation pressure

Avoid:

- SaaS landing page aesthetics
- LMS or classroom interface mood
- admin dashboards
- generic AI cards
- bright cheap yellow
- oversized pill buttons
- muddy brown-gold parchment
- decorative systems that compete with reading

## Workflow

1. Identify the surface being styled or reviewed.
2. Check whether Proust remains the visual and conceptual center.
3. Check whether typography, spacing, and contrast support sustained reading.
4. Flag any product-marketing, dashboard, LMS, or generic AI drift.
5. Recommend conservative style changes before proposing broad redesign.

## Rules

- Do not redesign the whole site unless explicitly asked.
- Do not introduce a one-note palette.
- Do not add decorative complexity to compensate for weak hierarchy.
- Do not make Language Training, System, or Daily visually more central than Proust.
- Do not use public-facing student, teacher, classroom, or instructor language.

## Output Shape

- Surface checked
- What supports reading
- Style drift risks
- Required corrections
- What to preserve
