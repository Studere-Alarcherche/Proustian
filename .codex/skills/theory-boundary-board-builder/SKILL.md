# Theory Boundary Board Builder

Use this skill when a passage needs theory lenses, scene pressures, or boundary checks.

## Purpose

A theory boundary board prevents theory from taking over the reading. It records what a lens clarifies, what it cannot decide, and where the passage resists it.

## Inputs

- Proust passage or plate
- theory lens I
- theory lens II when needed
- scene pressure
- boundary pressure

## Workflow

1. Name the passage problem before naming theory.
2. State what lens I clarifies.
3. State what lens II clarifies only if it adds pressure.
4. Identify what each lens cannot decide.
5. Return the reading to Proust's language, scene, and form.

## Output Shape

- Passage problem
- Lens I
- Lens I use
- Lens I limit
- Lens II
- Lens II use
- Lens II limit
- Scene pressure
- Boundary pressure
- Return to Proust

## Rules

- Deleuze is a method lens, not a top-level topic.
- Do not rewrite core theoretical claims without approval.
- Do not turn the board into a literature review.
- Do not add bibliography expansion unless asked.
