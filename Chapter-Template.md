# Chapter Template

## Chapter Metadata

```text
Part:
Chapter:
Title:
Chapter Function:
Primary Reading Move:
Passage ID:
Status:
```

## 1. Chapter Function

State the one action this chapter trains.

This should be concrete enough to test. Avoid broad goals such as "understand Proust's style."

## 2. Before You Begin

Prepare the reader for the action they will practice.

Include only the minimum orientation needed to enter the passage.

## 3. Passage Entrance

Introduce the passage source, location, and reason for selection.

Do not use fake passages. Do not replace source text with invented imitation.

## 3A. Original Text Citation and Annotation Layer

Use this layer only after a real passage boundary is verified.

Citation rule:

- Do not paste a long passage into the chapter.
- Use short citation anchors only.
- Keep each quoted anchor brief enough to function as a handle for close reading.
- Every quoted anchor must include source, version, and location metadata.

Annotation fields:

```text
Short citation anchor:
Citation function:
Grammar note:
Movement note:
CN recomposition note:
Writing transfer note:
Open question:
```

The purpose is to thicken the textbook through source-grounded annotation, not to replace the source text.

## 4. First Reading

Guide an initial reading of the passage.

The goal is to notice pressure before explaining it away.

## 5. Sentence Skeleton

Identify the grammatical structure that holds the sentence together.

For long or difficult sentences, separate:

- main clause
- subordinate clauses
- inserted phrases
- delayed arrivals
- reference points

## 6. Movement Map

Show how the sentence moves.

Map sequence, delay, return, pressure, or perceptual unfolding. The map should explain how the passage moves, not merely what it says.

## 7. English Style Studio

Train the English reading or style skill for the chapter.

Focus on syntax, rhythm, attention, pressure, or style formation.

## 8. Chinese Recomposition Studio

Train Chinese recomposition.

The task is not simply to produce a translation answer. It is to rebuild movement, perception, delay, rhythm, or time in Chinese.

## 9. Pattern Work

Name the reusable pattern or patterns extracted from the chapter.

Record candidate patterns in `Pattern-Bank.md` after the chapter is drafted.

## 10. Practice

Create exercises that require the reader to perform the chapter move.

Include a mix of:

- sentence skeleton practice
- movement map practice
- EN reading practice
- CN recomposition practice
- writing transfer practice

## 11. Self-Check

Give criteria for checking the work.

Self-check should focus on the trained action, not on one perfect answer.

## 12. What You Can Now Do

State the skill the reader can now perform.

Keep this practical and observable.

## 13. Next Chapter

Explain how the next chapter extends, complicates, or revises the current chapter's action.

Do not preview a whole theory system.
