## Golden entrances

- Madeleine｜memory / sensation / sign: a sensory event becomes a sign before it becomes an explanation.
- Vinteuil little phrase｜music / love sign / truth: music carries truth before it can be explained.
- Vermeer yellow wall｜visual attention / artistic truth: visual attention becomes a discipline of artistic truth.
- Bells / soundscape｜collective time: sound gathers private perception into collective time.
- Smell / atmosphere｜sensory history: smell and atmosphere mark social boundaries and historical subjectivity.
