# French Reading Textbook

The French Reading Textbook is the language precision layer of Proustian.

It is not a generic French course. It is a reading course built toward Proust.

French is the target language. English is the scaffold language. Chinese is not the main scaffold for this track.

The first planning horizon is A0 to B1, roughly 50-60 chapters, at about one chapter per week.

## Pedagogical Reliability

This is a targeted reading curriculum, not AI-generated improvisation.

It combines:

- reading-knowledge French textbook tradition
- classical-language reading textbook rigor
- evidence-informed instructed SLA principles
- input processing and form-meaning mapping
- Anki repetition
- self-tested diagnostic revision
- Proust-specific target pressure

Ordinary French textbooks serve general language ability. This textbook serves reading competence toward Proust. It may skip conversation, travel situations, and general communicative tasks, but it must not skip reading grammar, vocabulary recognition, function words, reference, tense, subordination, or long-sentence parsing.
