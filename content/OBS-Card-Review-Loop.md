# Card Review Loop

## Loop

1. Study a chapter or exercise.
2. Convert the knowledge point into a card.
3. Review the card.
4. Mark failed cards.
5. Diagnose the failure.
6. Rewrite the card or reread the source section.
7. Log the repair.

## Failure Types

- vocabulary
- form
- structure
- reference
- tense
- subordination
- expression

## Rule

Do not add more cards to cover a failed card until the failure type is named.
