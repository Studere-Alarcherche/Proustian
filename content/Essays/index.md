# Essays / Public Writing

This is the public articulation layer of Proustian.

Essays belong here only when they have become judgments, not when they are still raw capture. A note may begin privately as a fragment, receipt, reading pressure, or unfinished intuition; it enters this section only after it can stand in public as an argument, a scene of attention, or a finished piece of prose.

## Editorial Rule

No raw notes. No dumping ground. No automatic publication from the archive.

The Essays section should remain sparse if the writing has not matured. A quiet index of finished work is better than a crowded shelf of drafts pretending to be essays.

## Relation to Proustian

Proust is the center, not the limit. Public writing may move through literature, places, tools, coffee, travel, or method, but it should carry the same discipline: attention first, judgment second, publication last.
