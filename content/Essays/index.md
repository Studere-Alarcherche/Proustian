# Essays

Essays is the writing and public articulation layer of Proustian.

This is the only blog-like container. Do not create a separate Blog folder.

Essays may include finished essays, public fragments, method notes, reading reflections, translation reflections, and project essays. Not every essay needs to be final. A fragment may remain a fragment until it later joins a stronger constellation.

## Function

Essays exists to turn reading, training, and project movement into communicable form.

It should preserve:

- one sharp distinction;
- one passage insight;
- one method;
- one public-facing explanation;
- one local gain.
