# Vocabulary Progression

Vocabulary should support reading grammar and Proustian preparation.

Progression:

1. Cognates
2. False friends
3. Word families
4. Function words
5. Proust lexical fields
6. Proust-oriented micro-readings

## Principles

- Use high-yield reading vocabulary before topic vocabulary.
- Teach function words early and repeatedly.
- Mark false friends clearly.
- Use Proust-oriented vocabulary without forcing fake Proustian depth into A0 lessons.
- Keep vocabulary tied to sentence reading, not isolated memorization.
