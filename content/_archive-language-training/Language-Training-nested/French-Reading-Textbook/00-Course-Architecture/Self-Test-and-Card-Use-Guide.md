# Self-Test and Card Use Guide

Textbook teaches.

Self-Test checks.

Cards repeat.

Diagnostic Tutor repairs.

Training Logs preserve the learning trace.

## Self-Test

Use Self-Test after each textbook chapter.

Self-Test should check:

- sentence recognition
- subject and verb
- word order
- forms
- literal English control
- natural English paraphrase
- structural check
- cumulative review

## Cards

Cards should repeat only what has been taught.

Cards should be small:

- one form
- one function word
- one pattern
- one sentence skeleton
- one false friend

Do not make cards that require literary interpretation.

## Diagnostic Tutor

Use the Diagnostic Tutor when Self-Test or cards reveal a repeated gap.

The tutor identifies the gap, assigns repair practice, and records the result.
