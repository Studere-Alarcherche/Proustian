# Card Policy

Cards repeat what the textbook has already taught.

Cards do not teach new grammar by themselves.

Good cards are small, answerable, and tied to reading.

Avoid:

- large translation prompts
- literary interpretation cards
- cards with multiple possible answers
- cards that require grammar not yet introduced

Use cards for:

- forms
- articles
- gender and number
- function words
- false friends
- sentence skeletons
- Proust-oriented micro-patterns
