# Translation Training

Translation Training is part of Language Training, but it is not the French Reading Textbook.

It trains:

- Chinese-English paraphrase
- English-Chinese paraphrase
- literal control
- interpretive rewriting
- polished rewriting
- conceptual transfer
- expression precision
