# Language Training

Language Training is the language precision layer of Proustian.

It supports French reading, English scaffolded study, translation training, Anki-based repetition, diagnostic review, and eventual access to Proust's French source text.

Cards repeat.  
Self-test checks.  
Diagnostic Tutor repairs.
