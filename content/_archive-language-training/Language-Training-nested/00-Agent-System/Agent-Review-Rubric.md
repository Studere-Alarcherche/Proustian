# Agent Review Rubric

Use this rubric for Language Training reviews.

## Alignment

- Language Training remains part of Proustian.
- French remains the language precision layer.
- Translation Training is integrated but not confused with French Reading Textbook.
- The system supports Proust source-text access, Pattern Cards, terms, syntax, and the course evidence chain.

## Content-First Check

- No homepage work.
- No CSS work.
- No Quartz config work.
- No visual polish.
- No separate repository.

## French Reading Textbook Check

- A0-to-B1 progression is visible.
- English scaffold is clear.
- French is the target language.
- Essential reading grammar is preserved.
- Irrelevant general French is avoided where possible.
- Proust direction is present without fake A0 depth.

## Translation Training Check

- Chinese and English may both be used.
- The track trains paraphrase, transfer, rewriting, and expression.
- It does not merge into French textbook chapters.

## Verdicts

- Pass: ready to use.
- Revise: targeted fixes required.
- Reject: do not continue until repaired.
