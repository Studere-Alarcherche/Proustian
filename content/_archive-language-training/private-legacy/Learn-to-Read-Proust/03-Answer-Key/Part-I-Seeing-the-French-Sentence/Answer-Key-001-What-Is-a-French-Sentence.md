## Common Errors

- Translating before identifying the verb.
- Treating every noun as the subject.
- Missing the completion after `devient`.
- Reading a controlled sentence as a literary claim.
- Treating controlled training sentences as Proust quotations.
- Replacing English structural control with another scaffold language.
