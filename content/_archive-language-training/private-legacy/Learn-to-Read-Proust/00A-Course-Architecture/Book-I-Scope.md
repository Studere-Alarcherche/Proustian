# Book I Scope

Book I — Learning to See the French Sentence

Goal: train a zero-beginner reader to see French sentence structure before interpretation.

Book I teaches:

- what a French sentence is
- subject and verb
- nouns, gender, and number
- articles
- `être` and `avoir`
- regular present tense
- adjectives and agreement
- prepositions
- negation
- basic sentence reading
- English structural control

Book I is not yet direct Proust reading. It prepares the reader for direct Proust reading by building structural sight.

Proust-oriented vocabulary is useful, but it must not force fake depth into A0 lessons.
