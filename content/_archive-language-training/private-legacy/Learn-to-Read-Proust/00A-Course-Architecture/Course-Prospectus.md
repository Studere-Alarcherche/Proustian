# Course Prospectus

Learn to Read Proust is a self-study French reading course for a zero-beginner whose final target is Marcel Proust, _À la recherche du temps perdu_.

The course is not a general French course. It is also not a translation workbook or a literary essay project.

The course trains the reader to see French sentence structure before interpretation. Proust remains the final textual object, but early lessons must stay pedagogically flexible and structurally clear.

Book I begins with the simplest reading acts: finding the sentence, identifying the subject and verb, recognizing nouns and articles, and using English as a structural control language.

The course should be rigorous, cumulative, and workbook-driven.
