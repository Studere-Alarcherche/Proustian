# English Scaffold Policy

French is the target language and final textual object.

English is the scaffold language:

- explanations
- grammar labels
- literal control readings
- natural paraphrases
- comparison notes
- structural checks
- answer-key explanations

Chinese is not a learner-facing scaffold language in this course.

Chinese may appear only in `content/private/Learn-to-Read-Proust/07-Editor-Notes/`, or in a file explicitly marked as a private editor note.

Workbook labels should use:

- Literal English Control
- Natural English Paraphrase
- Structural Check
- English Reading Note

Avoid:

- Chinese paraphrase drills
- Chinese-French translation exercises
- translation-training workflows
- literary essay commentary inside A0 grammar lessons
