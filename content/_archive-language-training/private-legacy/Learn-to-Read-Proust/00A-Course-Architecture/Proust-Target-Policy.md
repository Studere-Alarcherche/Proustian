# Proust Target Policy

The final target is Marcel Proust, _À la recherche du temps perdu_, in French.

Early chapters do not need to read Proust directly. They should prepare the reader for Proust by training structural sight.

Use Proust-oriented vocabulary when it helps:

- memory
- time
- room
- sensation
- perception
- sentence movement
- narrator as a course-control label

Do not force fake Proustian depth into A0 lessons.

Model sentences may be controlled or adapted. They must be clearly marked as training sentences when they are not quotations.

Long Proust quotations should wait until the reader has enough grammar to parse them.
