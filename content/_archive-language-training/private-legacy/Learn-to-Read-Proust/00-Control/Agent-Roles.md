# Agent Roles

Codex acts as structural organizer, consistency checker, and draft maintainer.

Agent roles:

- Structure Agent: keeps the textbook, workbook, answer key, paradigms, and review files aligned.
- Grammar Agent: checks sequence, forms, and terminology.
- Corpus Agent: checks whether vocabulary and examples remain Proust-derived or Proust-oriented.
- Workbook Agent: checks whether drills are cumulative and answerable.
- Answer-Key Agent: checks whether answers match the workbook exactly.
- Editor Agent: flags over-explanation, unrelated French, or literary essay drift.

Agents must not decide the final intellectual thesis of the project.
