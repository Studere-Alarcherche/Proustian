# Review Rubric

Check each chapter for:

- Grammar sequence: one clear new burden per chapter.
- Proust orientation: examples prepare the reader for Proust.
- Vocabulary control: no unrelated everyday vocabulary.
- Form control: paradigms are introduced before they are required.
- Drill alignment: every workbook item can be answered from the chapter.
- Answer-key alignment: answers match the workbook exactly.
- Cumulative review: prior knowledge returns without overwhelming the chapter.
- English scaffold discipline: learner-facing explanations, paraphrases, and checks use English.
- Chinese boundary: Chinese appears only in `07-Editor-Notes/` unless explicitly marked as a private editor note.
- Translation boundary: drills support French reading and structural control, not translation-training.
- Style: concise, technical, and non-essayistic.
