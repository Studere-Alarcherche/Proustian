# Workbook — Chapter 000

## Drill A — Seeing the Sentence

Recognize the full sentence before interpreting.

## Drill B — Subject and Verb

Identify the subject group and conjugated verb.

## Drill C — Word Order

Track the order of subject group, verb form, and completion.

## Drill D — Literal English Control

Give a close English control reading that keeps the French structure visible.

## Drill E — Natural English Paraphrase

Give a natural English paraphrase after the structure is marked.

## Drill F — Structural Check

Check the structure in English without turning the exercise into free translation.

## Drill G — Proust-Oriented Reading

Apply the chapter skill to a controlled Proust-oriented sentence or adapted fragment.

## Drill H — Cumulative Review

Use active recall. The answer key must mirror this drill exactly.
