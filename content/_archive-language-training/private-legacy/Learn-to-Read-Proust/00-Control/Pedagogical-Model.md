# Pedagogical Model

The model follows the structural discipline of _Learn to Read Latin_ and _Learn to Read Greek_, adapted for a zero-beginner reader learning to read French toward Proust.

Each chapter should contain:

- a precise aim
- a reading target
- a grammar sequence
- forms or paradigms
- vocabulary
- model sentences
- guided parsing
- workbook drills
- an answer key
- cumulative review

The method is reading-first. The reader learns forms and syntax because they make French sentence structure visible.

Language hierarchy:

- French is the target language and final textual object.
- English is the scaffold language, explanation language, comparison language, and structural control language.
- Chinese is reserved for private editor notes only.

Translation is a tool, not the endpoint. English control readings should clarify French structure; they should not turn the workbook into translation training.

The endpoint is controlled reading: seeing the sentence, locating its structure, and eventually following the movement of memory, perception, and delay in Proust.
