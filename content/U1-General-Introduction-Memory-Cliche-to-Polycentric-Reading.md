# U1｜General Introduction: From the Memory Cliché to Polycentric Reading

U1 is not a signs unit. It is the general introduction that uses the memory cliché as a methodological hinge: from involuntary memory to competing reading lenses, from single-center interpretation to claim/problem chains, and finally back to Proust’s textual world.

## Position｜U1 as General Introduction

U1 establishes the course’s methodological tendency. It does not ask the reader to replace memory with signs, nor to choose Deleuze as the master key. It shows how the cliché “Proust is a novelist of memory” becomes the first pressure point from which the whole course opens.

### Not This｜Signs as Course Center

U1 should not make “signs” into a new sovereign center. Deleuze remains useful, but he is only one theoretical line among several.

### But This｜Memory Cliché as Methodological Hinge

The course begins with the force and insufficiency of involuntary memory, then unfolds multiple reading paths that return to the novel.

> **Core formulation:** U1 is a methodological overture. It explains how Proustian 2.0 reads through strong theoretical positions without allowing any one of them to own Proust.

## The Memory Cliché as Starting Pressure

The tradition of involuntary memory is not a weak opponent. It is powerful enough to become a recurring object of criticism, compression, displacement, and renewal. U1 must therefore begin by granting its force before showing why it cannot remain the only center.

### English Claim

Memory is powerful but insufficient.

Proust is often entered through lost time, involuntary memory, and the return of the personal past. This entry is legitimate, but incomplete. If memory becomes the first and final explanation, the novel’s errors, delays, social codes, musical pressures, visual fragments, and artistic discoveries are flattened.

### 中文判断

记忆是强传统，但不是最终中心。

非自愿记忆当然构成《追忆》中最具震动性的经验之一。但如果它成为唯一解释，小说中的误读、延宕、社交姿态、爱情迷误、音乐压力、视觉凝聚与艺术真理都会被压成“过去回来了”。

> U1 does not reject memory. It uses memory as the first hinge through which Proustian reading moves from a single strong tradition toward a polycentric method.

## Basic Literature Review as Method

The literature review in U1 should not be a bibliography dump. It should present reading positions: what each lens sees, what it risks, and how it returns to the Proustian text-world.

| Reading Position | What It Sees | What It Risks | Return to Text |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Memory Tradition** | Lost time returns through sensation, involuntary memory, and the sudden recovery of personal history. | Reducing the novel to recollection, nostalgia, or psychological recovery. | Ask what returns, and why the return is delayed, unstable, or transformed. |
| **Deleuze** | Signs force thought; the narrator learns through error, apprenticeship, and interpretation. | Flattening prose into taxonomy or turning Proust into Deleuze. | Ask whether signs clarify the passage, or whether classification weakens its literary pressure. |
| **Poulet** | Memory becomes space; moments and places condense into fields of vision. | Freezing narrative movement into static contemplation. | Ask what spatial image or mosaic-like arrangement the passage forms. |
| **Narrative Discourse** | Meaning emerges through delay, order, duration, frequency, voice, retrospection, and narrative arrangement. | Over-formalizing the text and losing the pressure of experience. | Ask how the passage becomes readable only through narrative structure. |
| **Visual / Music / Sensory Paths** | Proust transforms image, sound, smell, gesture, and atmosphere into literary form. | Drifting into art history, musicology, or sensory history without textual return. | Ask how the medium is absorbed by the novel rather than simply added to it. |

## Polycentric Reading｜No Center Does Not Mean No Judgment

U1 introduces a polycentric method. “No center” does not mean arbitrary wandering. It means no single theory is allowed to become sovereign. Each lens must become strong enough to make a claim, produce a problem, and submit itself to textual testing.

### Rule 1｜No single theory owns Proust.

Memory, signs, space, narrative delay, music, visuality, and sensory history are all possible entrances, not final masters.

### Rule 2｜Every lens must become strong.

A weak lens produces decoration. A strong lens produces a claim, a problem, and a pressure against other readings.

### Rule 3｜The text tests every claim.

A theory is useful only when it returns to a sentence, scene, rhythm, object, gesture, sound, or misrecognition.

## Claim / Problem Chain

This is the central writing method for U1. The goal is not to list themes, but to unfold claims until their problems, counterclaims, limits, and textual returns become visible.

| Step | Action | Function |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Claim | State what a reading position asserts. |
| 2 | Passage | Attach the claim to a concrete Proust scene. |
| 3 | Problem | Show what the claim cannot easily explain. |
| 4 | Counter-Claim | Let another lens answer or resist it. |
| 5 | Limit | Expose where the second lens also begins to fail. |
| 6 | Return | Go back to the prose and form a judgment. |

> **Method principle:** The more fully a claim/problem chain is unfolded, the more testable, comparable, and recoverable the reading becomes. This is U1’s version of rigor.

## Kite-Line Reading｜Textual Spine and Theoretical Lines

The hand-drawn model becomes the course’s visual method: theory flies outward from the text, reaches its horizon, dissolves, and returns.

### English Formulation

The text is the spine.

Theories are lines released above it. Each line may fly outward, rise, diverge, and open a new field of vision. But every line must return to the Proustian text-world.

### 中文表述

文本是脊骨。

理论是在文本脊骨上方放飞的线束。每条理论线都可以飞出、分叉、升高，但最终必须重新降落到普鲁斯特的句子、场景、感官与叙事运动之中。

> Proustian 2.0 does not reject theory. It releases theory from the text, lets it fly to its horizon, watches it dissolve, and then returns to the novel and to the reader’s own perception.

## Reading Action｜U1 Trains Method, Not Sign Classification

The reading action no longer begins by asking “what kind of sign is this?” It begins with the passage and asks what pressure appears. Signs are one possible lens, not the center.

1. Start with a Proust passage, not a theory.
2. Identify the dominant pressure: memory, error, delay, image, sound, social gesture, sensation, or artistic form.
3. Choose one provisional lens: memory, signs, space, narrative discourse, visuality, music, or sensory history.
4. Ask what this lens makes visible in the passage.
5. Ask what this lens fails to see, weakens, or over-systematizes.
6. Let another lens answer, resist, or intensify the first claim.
7. Return to the prose: sentence, rhythm, scene, gesture, object, sound, or image.
8. Produce a readerly judgment: what did this passage teach the reader to see?

## Core Lenses Opened by U1

U1 introduces several strong reading lenses. Each one is provisional. Each one must return to the passage.

### Memory

- What lost past returns?
- What sensory shock triggers it?
- Why is return not enough?

### Signs

- What pressure forces interpretation?
- What does the narrator misread?
- Where does apprenticeship begin?

### Space / Vision

- What spatial image forms?
- How do moments and places become a mosaic?
- What does vision compress?

### Narrative Discourse

- What is delayed?
- Who knows what, and when?
- How does narrative order make meaning possible?

### Music / Visuality

- How does another art enter the novel?
- Does the novel imitate it or transform it?
- What new form of truth appears?

### Sensory / Social Space

- How do smell, sound, room, salon, or street produce social time?
- What historical pressure enters private experience?

## Scene Anchors as Test Cases

These are not proof examples for a single theory. They are testing grounds where multiple lenses compete and return.

### Madeleine

Memory, sensation, sign, habit, delay, and narrative reorganization.

### Swann / Odette

Love, jealousy, deception, interpretation, social signs, and misreading.

### Names of Places

Fantasy, expectation, disappointment, space, language, and desire.

### Vinteuil

Music, love, ineffable truth, repetition, and artistic transformation.

### Vermeer

Visual attention, art sign, mortality, color, detail, and judgment.

## Transition to U2–U6

U1 opens the whole course. It does not close itself around Deleuze or signs. Its output is a method that can move into later units.

| Question Opened in U1 | Later Unit | Course Function |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Why is memory powerful but insufficient? | U2｜Memory / Time / Space | Turns recollection into structure, delay, space, and passage-level reading. |
| How do signs, errors, and forced thought train the reader? | U1 bridge + U2 | Provides a portable method without making Deleuze the center. |
| How does music become a path toward truth? | U3｜Music / Ineffable | Moves from emotional motif to artistic and novelistic structure. |
| How does seeing become attention and judgment? | U4｜Visuality / Gaze | Reads visual fragments as pressures on perception and form. |
| How do spaces and sensations produce social time? | U5｜Sensory History / Social Space | Links private sensation with historical and social form. |
| How can reading become a reader-facing structure? | U6｜Writing Apparatus | Converts U1–U5 into pages, cards, labs, and course architecture. |

## Final Judgment

The center is not memory, not signs, not Deleuze, not Poulet, and not any single theory. The center is the disciplined effort to approach Proust’s novel through competing, testable, returnable reading paths.
