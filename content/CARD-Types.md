# Card Types

## Form Card

Prompt: identify a form.

Example: `revient`

Answer: present, 3rd person singular of `revenir`; returns / comes back.

## Article + Noun Card

Prompt: identify gender and number.

Example: `la chambre`

Answer: feminine singular; the room.

## Function Word Card

Prompt: give the structural role.

Example: `de`

Answer: relation / source / possession, depending on context.

## Pattern Card

Prompt: parse a micro-sentence.

Example: `Le souvenir revient.`

Answer: subject group `le souvenir`; verb `revient`; no completion.

## False Friend Card

Prompt: correct the misleading expectation.

Example: `actuellement`

Answer: currently, not actually.
