# Self-Test Policy

Self-tests are light verification, not heavy workbook architecture.

They should check whether the reader can apply the chapter's core skill without turning the course into dead drills.

## Rules

- Test only what the textbook has taught.
- Keep prompts short.
- Use English structural control in the French Reading Textbook track.
- Send repeated failures to [[DIAG-Policy|Diagnostic Tutor]].
