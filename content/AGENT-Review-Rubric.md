# Agent Review Rubric

Use this rubric for Language Training reviews.

## Alignment

- Language Training remains part of Proustian.
- French remains the language precision layer.
- Translation Training is integrated but not confused with French Reading Textbook.
- The system supports Proust source-text access, Pattern Cards, terms, syntax, and the course evidence chain.

## Content-First Check

- No homepage work.
- No CSS work.
- No Quartz config work.
- No visual polish.
- No separate repository.

## French Reading Textbook Check

- A0-to-B1 progression is visible.
- English scaffold is clear.
- French is the target language.
- Essential reading grammar is preserved.
- Irrelevant general French is avoided where possible.
- Proust direction is present without fake A0 depth.
- The chapter follows a recognized reading-language sequence.
- The chapter trains one observable reading action.
- Morphology and syntax are explicit when needed.
- The chapter distinguishes form, function, and meaning.
- The chapter includes card-conversion potential.

## Pedagogical Reliability Check

- The chapter names its source tradition or pedagogical basis.
- The learning target is clear and observable.
- The self-test can expose a real gap.
- Failure can revise the chapter, cards, or self-test.
- The Diagnostic Tutor can classify likely failure.
- The editor has self-tested the chapter before it is marked stable.

## Skill Compliance

- Did the chapter invoke the relevant skills?
- Are outputs compatible with the required skill formats?
- Are card targets reviewable?
- Are failure signals visible?
- Is Proustian alignment specific?
- Did vocabulary work use the correct vocabulary skill rather than a generic list?
- Did the diagnostic step classify failure instead of prescribing blanket repetition?

## Translation Training Check

- Chinese and English may both be used.
- The track trains paraphrase, transfer, rewriting, and expression.
- It does not merge into French textbook chapters.

## Verdicts

- Pass: ready to use.
- Revise: targeted fixes required.
- Reject: do not continue until repaired.

No chapter may receive Pass if it is not auditable under [[AGENT-Pedagogical-Audit-Principles|Pedagogical Audit Principles]].
