# English Scaffold Policy

French is the target language and final textual object.

English is the scaffold language:

- explanations
- grammar labels
- literal control readings
- natural paraphrases
- comparison notes
- structural checks
- answer-key explanations

Chinese is not the main scaffold for the French Reading Textbook. It may appear only in meta/project notes if necessary.

Required learner-facing labels:

- Literal English Control
- Natural English Paraphrase
- Structural Check
- English Reading Note

Avoid:

- Chinese paraphrase drills
- Chinese-French translation exercises inside French textbook chapters
- translation-training workflows inside French textbook chapters
- literary essay commentary inside A0 grammar lessons
