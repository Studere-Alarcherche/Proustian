# Control Center｜Proustian Dashboard

This is the user's operational center for Proustian. It is not a Codex dashboard and not an internal Projects module. It is the current status cockpit where active areas, progress, next actions, and boundaries are made visible.

It is not the intellectual center of Proustian. Proust / Recherche remains the intellectual center. But Control Center is the operational center for deciding what is active, what is paused, what has been completed, and what should happen next.

Read Proust -> Learn to Read Proust -> From Proust to World Literature -> Return to Writing
