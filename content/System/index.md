# System

The system exists to serve reading. It keeps orientation, preserves current state, and prevents the archive garden from becoming a pile of disconnected intentions.

It is not the public course and not the center of Proustian. The center remains reading, language precision, judgment, and writing.

## Boundary

There is no Projects module here. Projects stay outside Obsidian. The System folder should answer practical questions: where to begin, what is current, what is paused, and what must not be mixed into the reading surface.
