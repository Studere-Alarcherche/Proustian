# SKILL-False-Friend-Alert

## Purpose

Create a warning system for French words that mislead English readers.

This skill prevents cognate recognition from becoming uncontrolled guessing.

## When to Use

- Chapter 002
- vocabulary banks
- recognition cards
- diagnostic repair after misreading
- review of English-looking French words

## Inputs

- French word
- likely wrong English guess
- correct reading meaning
- short example
- target diagnostic category

## Procedure

1. Identify false friend.
2. State the misleading English guess.
3. State the correct reading meaning.
4. Give one short example.
5. Convert into error-correction card.
6. Add to false friend bank.

Core examples:

- actuellement ≠ actually; means currently / at present
- attendre ≠ attend; means to wait for
- ignorer ≠ ignore; means not to know
- sensible ≠ sensible; means sensitive
- éventuellement ≠ eventually; means possibly / if necessary
- librairie ≠ library; means bookstore

## Output Format

## False Friend Alert

| French | Wrong Guess | Correct Reading | Example | Card Target |
|---|---|---|---|---|

## Audit Questions

- Is the likely wrong guess explicitly named?
- Is the correct reading meaning short and accurate?
- Is the example simple enough for the level?
- Can the item become an error-correction card?
- Is the failure diagnosable as a false friend gap?
- Does the explanation avoid long vocabulary essays?

## Failure Signals

- no warning marker
- wrong English explanation
- overlong explanation
- no error-correction card
- no diagnostic category
- encourages distrust of all cognates rather than controlled guessing
