# Translation Training Workflow

Translation Training is part of Language Training, but it is not the French Reading Textbook.

## Purpose

It trains:

- Chinese-English paraphrase
- English-Chinese paraphrase
- literal control
- interpretive rewriting
- polished rewriting
- conceptual transfer
- expression precision

## Workflow

1. Select a short passage.
2. Produce a literal version.
3. Produce an interpretive version.
4. Produce a polished version.
5. Compare conceptual loss.
6. Convert useful mistakes into cards.
7. Log the training result.

## Relation to French Reading Textbook

Translation Training supports general language precision and expression.

French Reading Textbook supports reading French toward Proust.

They are sibling tracks under Language Training.

They should not be merged.
