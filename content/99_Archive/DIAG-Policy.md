# Diagnostic Policy

The Diagnostic Tutor repairs gaps revealed by Self-Test, cards, or reading attempts.

It does not replace the textbook.

It should identify:

- the failed skill
- the likely cause
- the smallest repair exercise
- whether the reader can continue
- whether to reread, add cards, rewrite cards, or move forward

Repairs should stay narrow and practical.

## Failure Categories

- Vocabulary Gap
- Form Gap
- Function Gap
- Word Order Gap
- Reference Gap
- Tense Gap
- Subordination Gap
- Premature Interpretation

## Revision Rule

If failed cards or failed self-tests repeat, the system must revise the weakest link:

- chapter explanation
- model sentence
- card wording
- self-test prompt
- diagnostic category
