## 01. Poulet / Spatial Topology — The Panic of Localization

**Core pressure:** "When waking in the dark, why is the narrator's primary terror spatial rather than temporal? How does the body attempt to construct a room before the mind can remember the year?"

### Questions

1. Observe the ribs and thighs reconstructing the geometry of old bedrooms. How does Proust show that space is not a given container, but something that must be painfully built by physical memory?
   - 身体记忆的建筑学：肉体如何先于意识醒来？

2. Consider the feeling of "drifting in space." Why is finding the "little gate" in the mind's eye equivalent to recovering lost time?
   - 漂浮的空间：为何“我在哪里”比“现在何时”更令人恐慌？

3. Examine the concept of "wavering places" (Poulet). How does the room spin like a kaleidoscope and settle only after localization is achieved?
   - 摇曳的地点：意识锚定之前的空间万花筒。

4. What does the narrator's ignorance of *where* he is tell us about Proust's view of human identity being anchored essentially in spatial dimensions?
   - 空间的本体论：没有地点的“我”是否存在？

5. How does this visceral spatial panic disrupt the idea of a stable "Self" continuing seamlessly across time?
   - 自我的断裂：睡眠如何摧毁了连续的主体性幻觉？

## 02. Genette / Narrative Frequency — The Iterative Prison

**Core pressure:** "What I call *narrative frequency*, that is, the relations of frequency (or, more simply, of repetition) between the narrative and the diegesis..." — Gérard Genette.


 How does Proust manipulate this exact relation (using the imperfect tense) to swallow singular events into a generalized loop of habit?

### Questions

1. Find the transition in Combray where a specific Sunday morning dissolves into 'all Sundays'. What does this grammatical blurring do to our sense of safety and narrative progression?
   - 时间的模糊：一个具体的周日是如何不知不觉吞噬了所有周日的？这种语法上的“晕染”如何让读者丧失了时间前进的方向感？

2. How does Aunt Léonie's 'jog-trot' represent the absolute victory of the Iterative over the Singulative?
   - 莱奥妮姑妈的钟摆：她那不变的日常“小步快跑”如何构成了一种拒绝例外的防御机制？

3. Analyze the phrase "For a long time I used to go to bed early." How does this set a trap of endless repetition before any singular story even begins?
   - 开篇的陷阱：“长久以来”这个短语如何在故事真正开始前，就抽空了时间前进的动力，将叙事锁进了一个无限循环？

4. What happens structurally when a "Singulative" event (a sudden exception, like Swann's unexpected visit) breaks into the "Iterative" routine? Does the prose suddenly dilate or contract?
   - 破裂的日常：当一个偶然事件（比如斯万的突然造访）刺破“迭代叙事”的硬壳时，句法结构是突然膨胀了还是收缩了？

5. If habit forms a "crust" over time, how does the reader's experience of reading stagnate intentionally alongside the characters' lives?
   - 阅读的停滞：如果习惯在时间上结了一层“硬痂”，读者的阅读体验是如何被这种庞大的时间机器故意催眠和拖慢的？

6. Consider the "Pseudo-Iterative." Are scenes like the long conversations in Combray actually too detailed to have happened repeatedly, yet presented as if they were habits?
   - 伪迭代的幻觉：贡布雷那些长篇大论的对话是否详细到根本不可能“每天如此发生”，但叙述者却偏要将它们伪装成一种习惯？

## 03. Deleuze / Sensuous Signs — The Violence of the Madeleine

**Core pressure:** "Deleuze claims the Madeleine is not about 'memory'. It is a sign-event that inflicts a violent, unexplainable joy. Why is memory actually secondary to this shock?"

### Questions

1. Track the precise moment the narrator tastes the tea. Why does the profound joy arrive *before* any image of Combray is remembered?
   - 先于画面的狂喜：感官冲击为何领先于理性认知？

2. Why does Proust call the madeleine experience a "failure" of interpretation in its initial stages? What is postponed?
   - 解释的失败：为何找到“原因”反而意味着符号的降级？

3. Contrast voluntary memory (snapshots) with involuntary memory (the tea). Why does Deleuze argue involuntary memory still falls short of pure Art?
   - 快照与复活：无意记忆为何仍然不是最终的真理？

4. Trace the physical expansion: from a drop of tea to the unfolding of an entire town. How is this an act of deciphering a "hieroglyph"?
   - 空间的折叠与展开：一滴茶水如何吐出一座小镇？

5. If the sensuous sign "forces thought," what exactly forces the narrator to stop and analyze the taste rather than simply enjoying the cake?
   - 被强迫的思想：快乐如何转化为了不可遏制的破译冲动？

## 04. Poulet / Spatial Discontinuity — The Law of Closed Vases

**Core pressure:** "Why does Proust separate the Guermantes Way and the Méséglise Way so absolutely? How does this spatial fragmentation act as a blueprint for the isolation of human souls?"

### Questions

1. How does the narrator's inability to imagine the two Ways connecting reflect a deeper epistemological limit about knowing the 'other'?
   - 不可通约的空间：两条小路为何永远无法在脑海中交汇？

2. How does the absolute spatial separation of the Guermantes Way and the Méséglise Way construct two entirely different aesthetic and social universes?
   - 空间的阶级性：不同的方向如何孕育出不同的人格？

3. Examine the concept of "distance" or "negative space" in Poulet. What fills the gap between these isolated "monads"?
   - 负空间的张力：隔离不同地点的巨大虚无是什么？

4. If places are "closed vases," how does this map onto the impossibility of truly possessing another person (like Albertine)?
   - 地理的隐喻：封闭的地点与无法穿透的他者灵魂。

5. Why must the two ways remain separated in childhood, only to be juxtaposed (not merged) much later by the final work of art?
   - 艺术的并置：最终的空间是如何在写作中被重新排列的？

## 05. Landy / Optical Illusion — Martinville and Perspective

**Core pressure:** "How does the optical illusion of the moving steeples turn perception into a demand for writing? Why does Marcel project his own mind onto the landscape?"

### Questions

1. Analyze the carriage ride. How does physical displacement force the static 'closed vases' of space to overlap, causing the steeples to shift like a living organism?
   - 马车上的动理学：运动如何赋予死寂风景以生命？

2. Why does this specific optical illusion relieve Marcel of his despair about never becoming a writer?
   - 解除写作焦虑：错觉为何比“真实的风景”更具有文学价值？

3. How does the carriage's movement expose the falsity of a fixed, objective reality, introducing the "sinuosity" of subjective perception?
   - 客观现实的破产：蜿蜒的视角如何重组了世界的样貌？

4. How does the "turning" of the steeples prefigure the "turning fires of jealousy" later experienced with Odette and Albertine?
   - 风景与嫉妒的同构：钟楼的位移如何预演了情感的猜疑？

5. If the landscape changes faces based on the spectator's movement, what does this imply about the "truth" of any given scene in Proust?
   - 视角的暴政：如果一切都在移动，真相栖身于何处？

   - 实体的幻灭：为何抵达威尼斯的那一刻，威尼斯就死去了？

   - 想象与记忆的对立：向前的投射与向后的拉扯。

5. Analyze the mechanism of desire here: Why is the desire for a place fueled more by its linguistic inaccessibility than its actual geography?

## 07. Landy / Self-Deception — The Slipshod Detective

2. Examine Swann's "will to ignorance." How does he intentionally construct a flawed investigation to avoid a truth that would destroy his happiness?

5. Contrast Swann's aestheticization of Odette (seeing her as a Botticelli painting) with his jealousy. How is the former a defense mechanism?

   - 囚禁的失败：为何把她锁在房间里，她依然散发着外面的风景？

2. Why does Deleuze argue that loving someone means loving the "unknown worlds" implicated within them?

   - 断裂的爱人：无数个平行的阿尔贝蒂娜如何让统一的主体破产？

   - 谎言的引力：为何破译谎言的痛苦，反而构成了爱情的生命力？

## 09. Genette / Narrative Order — Anachrony and the Knowing Voice

**Core pressure:** "How does the older, retrospective 'Narrating I' intrude upon the ignorant 'Experiencing I'? What happens to the tone when a prolepsis reveals a tragedy before it happens?"

### Questions

1. Locate a sentence where the narrator says 'I did not yet know that...' How does this gap between the hero's ignorance and the narrator's knowledge create an 'apprenticeship'?
   - 后见之明的悲剧：叙述者与体验者之间的信息差。

2. Locate an instance of "Analepsis" (flashback). How does jumping backward disrupt the illusion of a naturally unfolding chronological life?
   - 闪回的突袭：过去的片段如何像陨石一样砸进当前的叙事？

3. How does the older narrator's "polymodality" – shifting between intense subjective feeling and god-like omniscience – destabilize the text?
   - 多重视角的重影：一边深陷痛苦，一边如上帝般俯瞰全局。

4. Examine the tragic irony of Prolepsis. When the narrator hints at Albertine's future death, how does it change our reading of her present actions?
   - 预叙的阴影：提前宣告死亡，如何改变了我们对日常对话的感受？

5. Why does Proust make the "time of the narrating" (the act of writing the book) so visible, rather than hiding it behind the "time of the story"?
   - 写作现场的暴露：为什么作者刻意让我们看到他在“编织”时间？

## 10. Synthesis / Landy — Style as Spiritual Exercise

**Core pressure:** "Ultimately, how do Proust's notoriously long, winding sentences function not merely as description, but as a cognitive training apparatus for the reader?"

### Questions

1. Look at any 10-line sentence. How does the suspension of the main clause, the nesting of metaphors, and the delayed resolution force the reader to experience the delayed 'truth of signs'?
   - 句法中的延迟：过长的从句如何让读者亲身体验“等待真相”的痛苦？

2. How does the extreme length and nested structure of a Proustian sentence force the reader to experience memory directly, rather than just reading about it?
   - 迷宫般的修辞：阅读的费力本身就是一种对记忆重组的模拟。

3. Landy argues that Marcel's maxims are often subjective self-delusions. How does Proust's *style* signal to the reader when a maxim should be doubted?
   - 反讽的文体标记：我们如何从夸张的语气中听出叙述者的不真诚？

4. Consider the act of juxtaposition in the final artwork (Poulet). How does style weave together the disparate "closed vases" into a single text?
   - 缝合碎片的针线：风格是如何将互不相干的地点并置在一起的？

5. Why does Landy insist that a unified Self is an aesthetic creation? How does the very act of writing the *Recherche* become Marcel's "Self-Creation"?
   - 自我创造的终局：为什么只有通过写作，散落的主体才能合而为一？
