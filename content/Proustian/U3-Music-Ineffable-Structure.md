## 01. Deleuze / Art Signs — The Immaterial Keyboard

**Core pressure:** "Why is art the ultimate realm of signs? Deleuze argues that while sensuous signs (the madeleine) are 'half sheathed in the object bearing them,' the signs of art are dematerialized. The piano is merely the spatial image of an entirely spiritual entity."

### Questions

1. Examine the phrase "sine materia." How does Proust describe Vinteuil's music as existing independently of the instruments that produce it?
   - 非物质的显现：为何乐器只是召唤音乐“精灵”的祭坛？

2. Why does Deleuze claim that breaking the music down into "five notes very close together" (3 + 2) explains absolutely nothing about its power?
   - 分析的失效：为什么物理和乐理的拆解无法触及音乐的“本质”？

3. How do the performers act less like musicians and more like "hierophants" executing a supernatural rite for the phrase to appear?
   - 降神仪式：演奏者如何退位成为不可言说之物的祭司？

4. If sensuous signs (taste, smell) rely on material contiguity, how does the little phrase transcend the limits of matter entirely?
   - 超越感官：艺术符号为何比玛德莱娜蛋糕更彻底地击碎了唯物主义？

## 02. Nattiez / Musical Perception — The Three Stages of Listening

**Core pressure:** "Nattiez outlines a three-stage progression in Proust’s musical apprehension: first an indistinct impression, then an intellectual (linguistic/descriptive) mapping, and finally, a purification into pure sonorous form. How does the listener surpass the 'descriptive' trap?"

### Questions

1. Locate Swann's first hearing of the Sonata. How does Proust describe the music initially as a "pure magma of sound" or an indistinct impression?
   - 声音的岩浆：在理性介入之前，音乐如何以混沌的形态冲击听觉？

2. Why is the second stage (attaching specific ideas or images to musical motifs) considered a "false trail" or an illusion of understanding?
   - 语言的陷阱：为何将音乐翻译成具体的画面（如“月光”或“森林”）是一种误读？

3. How does the narrator in 'The Captive' finally reach the third stage with the Septet, perceiving it as a "pure play of sonorous forms"?
   - 纯粹的声响：在《女囚》中，叙述者如何最终越过了内容的束缚，抵达了纯形式的深邃？

4. How does this three-stage progression of listening mirror the larger structure of Marcel's quest for Truth?
   - 认知的同构：听懂一段音乐的过程，为何恰恰是整部《追忆》“寻找真理”的缩影？

## 03. Deleuze / Love Signs — Swann's Reduction of the Infinite

**Core pressure:** "Swann degrades the art sign into a love sign. Instead of letting Vinteuil’s little phrase lead him to universal essence, he annexes it, turning it into the mere 'national anthem' of his affair with Odette. How does love blind the aesthetic sense?"

### Questions

1. Examine the moment Swann associates the little phrase with Odette's face. How does this act of "subjective compensation" effectively kill the music's transcendent power?
   - 主观的降级：斯万将乐句与奥黛特的脸绑定，是如何“杀死”了这首曲子的？

2. Why does Swann prefer the "love sign" (which brings him jealousy and pain) over the "art sign" (which brings pure joy)? What is the gravity of love?
   - 爱情的引力：为何斯万宁愿在嫉妒的痛苦中打转，也不愿追随艺术走向升华？

3. How does the phrase act as an "alibi" for Swann, allowing him to feel profound emotions without actually confronting the truth of his wasted life?
   - 情感的代偿：小乐句如何成了斯万逃避现实空虚的“不在场证明”？

4. Contrast Swann's failure with the Sonata to the Narrator's success with the Septet. What does Marcel do differently?
   - 对比与超越：同样是凡特伊的音乐，叙述者在《女囚》中做对了什么，而斯万却做错了？

## 04. Jankélévitch / Doing vs. Saying — The Charm and the Alibi

**Core pressure:** "Music is an act of Doing (poiesis), not an act of Saying (logos). It enchants without instructing. How does Jankélévitch's refusal of 'musical hermeneutics' challenge our desire to constantly 'translate' Vinteuil's music into literary ideas?"

### Questions

1. Jankélévitch argues music has no "dictionary." Where in the text does Proust emphasize the frustration of trying to map words onto the little phrase?
   - 无词典的语言：普鲁斯特在哪里描写了“词不达意”的音乐聆听之苦？

2. If music doesn't "say" anything, how does it "act" upon Swann or Marcel? How is its power drastic and immediate rather than communicative?
   - 作为行动的音乐：如果音乐不传递信息，它是如何直接“袭击”听者的神经的？

3. Consider the "performer as re-creator." How does the act of playing the music (Doing) supersede the act of analyzing it (Knowing)?
   - 弹奏即重塑：为何音乐的真理存在于演奏的“动作”中，而不是评论的“文本”里？

4. Does Proust ultimately side with the "ineffable" mystery of music, or does he stubbornly try to enclose it in the prose of his novel?
   - 文学的野心：普鲁斯特是否承认了音乐的不可言说，抑或他试图用文学来吞噬音乐？

## 05. Nattiez / Schopenhauer — The Will and the Idea

**Core pressure:** "Unlike painting or literature, which copy the phenomena of the world (Mimesis), Schopenhauer argues that music expresses the 'Will' directly. How does Proust adopt this metaphysical hierarchy to make music the ultimate redemptive model?"

### Questions

1. In 'The Captive', how does Marcel elevate Vinteuil's music above the visual arts? Why is sound considered "deeper" than images?
   - 超越视觉：在《女囚》中，马塞尔为何认为音乐比绘画更能触及宇宙的底色？

2. How does music serve as a "communication of souls" that bypasses the deception of language and worldly signs?
   - 灵魂的直连：音乐如何绕开了语言的谎言，实现了人与人之间唯一真实的沟通？

3. Nattiez notes that for Schopenhauer, the listener becomes a "pure, will-less, painless subject of knowledge." Does Marcel achieve this state while listening?
   - 无痛的主体：在音乐响起时，马塞尔是否真的摆脱了嫉妒与意志的折磨，化身为纯粹的认知者？

4. If music is the direct expression of the Will, why must Proust (the novelist) translate it back into words? Is literature ultimately jealous of music?
   - 文学的嫉妒：如果音乐已经是最高形式，普鲁斯特写这本小说的终极动力是否是对音乐的模仿与僭越？

## 06. Jankélévitch / Spatial Mirage — The Illusion of Architecture

**Core pressure:** "We constantly describe music as having 'structures,' 'lines,' and 'masses'—comparing it to a cathedral. Jankélévitch warns that this spatialization is an optical idol that kills the pure temporal becoming of music. Where does Proust fall into this trap, and where does he escape it?"

### Questions

1. Locate passages where Swann or Marcel describes Vinteuil's music using architectural metaphors (building, arches, foundations). What is gained and lost in this translation?
   - 听觉的建筑化：用“拱门”和“地基”来形容乐句，掩盖了音乐的什么本质？

2. How does the "diffluence" (flowing away) of time make music impossible to possess simultaneously?
   - 流逝的残酷：音乐无法像画作那样被“一眼看全”，这种时间性带来了怎样的占有焦虑？

3. Jankélévitch calls spatial transposition "vision layered upon hearing." How does Proust's emphasis on memory inherently spatialize the temporal flow of the sonata?
   - 记忆的冻结作用：普鲁斯特试图“记住”乐句的冲动，是否本质上是在将流动的音乐空间化、静止化？

4. Does the metaphor of the "invisible cathedral" elevate music, or does it betray music's true nature as an ephemeral vibration?
   - 隐形大教堂：这是一个赞美的隐喻，还是对音乐纯粹时间性的背叛？

## 07. Proust / Social performance — The Deafness of the Salon

**Core pressure:** "While music contains ineffable truth, it is played in the most banal, worldly settings. How does Proust contrast the transcendent power of Vinteuil’s music with the snobbery, empty formalism, and literal 'deafness' of Mme Verdurin and M. de Charlus?"

### Questions

1. Observe Mme Verdurin listening to music. How are her physical reactions (migraines, burying her face) a grotesque pantomime of genuine aesthetic appreciation?
   - 沙龙的哑剧：维尔迪兰夫人听音乐时的头痛与痉挛，如何沦为了一种社交表演？

2. How does Charlus use musical patronage (Morel) not for the sake of art, but as an instrument of social domination and sexual control?
   - 权力的乐器：夏尔吕斯如何将高雅的音乐赞助，扭曲为阶级碾压与情欲控制的工具？

3. Why does Proust place the profound revelation of the Septet in the exact center of a vicious, superficial salon gathering? What does this juxtaposition achieve?
   - 神圣与庸俗的并置：最纯粹的艺术启示，为何偏偏发生在最虚伪的社交场中？

4. How do "worldly signs" (Deleuze) operate as a necessary friction against which the "art signs" must struggle to be heard?
   - 社交符号的噪音：在满屋子漫不经心的闲聊中，艺术符号如何艰难地穿透世俗的屏蔽？

## 08. Jankélévitch / Inexpressive Espressivo — The Mirage of Development

**Core pressure:** "We are conditioned by rhetoric to hear 'development' in a sonata—a logical argument moving from A to B. But Jankélévitch suggests music is actually about 'Reprise': returning to the same point, differently. How does Vinteuil's little phrase enact Reprise rather than linear Development?"

### Questions

1. Track the recurrences of the little phrase. Does it "conclude" an argument like a syllogism, or does it simply return like a ghost or a familiar deity?
   - 并非三段论：小乐句的反复出现，是逻辑的推演，还是幽灵般的盘旋？

2. How does the concept of Repetition (Deleuze/Jankélévitch) replace the concept of Progress in listening to Proust's music?
   - 重复的魔法：为何在普鲁斯特的音乐体验中，“重现”比“进步”更重要？

3. Jankélévitch claims "one becomes a kithara player in playing the kithara." How does this relate to Marcel's gradual, iterative process of learning to 'hear' the Septet?
   - 做中学的学徒期：马塞尔如何通过一遍遍地被音乐“穿透”，才最终学会了“聆听”？

4. What is the "inexpressive Espressivo"? How can music be overwhelmingly emotional yet devoid of any definable, translatable sentiment?
   - 无表情的表情：音乐如何做到饱含极其强烈的情感，却又拒绝表达任何具体的“意思”？

## 09. Proust / The Septet — The Red of the Morning

**Core pressure:** "The Sonata was lunar, silvery, and melancholy. The Septet is terrestrial, red, and overwhelmingly joyous. How does Marcel’s encounter with the Septet in 'The Captive' mark the leap from the illusions of love to the brutal, necessary truth of Art?"

### Questions

1. Contrast the descriptive colors of the Sonata (violet, lunar, twilight) with the Septet (scarlet, dawn, rustic). What shift in Vinteuil's soul does this represent?
   - 色彩的隐喻：从奏鸣曲的“淡紫”到七重奏的“猩红”，凡特伊的灵魂经历了怎样的蜕变？

2. How does Marcel recognize Vinteuil's "unique signature" underlying both the timid sonata and the triumphant septet?
   - 唯一的指纹：马塞尔如何从截然不同的曲风中，辨认出属于凡特伊的那个不可替代的“音色”？

3. Marcel initially finds the joyous motif of the bells "almost ugly" and "laboriously earth-bound." Why must artistic truth sometimes appear brutal before it appears beautiful?
   - 丑陋的真实：为何七重奏中那个如敲击桌面般粗糙的欢乐动机，最初让马塞尔感到抗拒？

4. How does the Septet serve to retrospectively re-evaluate everything Marcel thought he knew about Albertine and his own capacity for creation?
   - 艺术的审判：七重奏的光芒，如何反过来照亮并重估了马塞尔那充满谎言的现实生活？

## 10. Nattiez / Denial of Origin — The Effaced Creator

**Core pressure:** "Nattiez notes that Proust tries to make Vinteuil's music seem like a 'pure object of philosophical contemplation,' independent of history or the labor of composition. Why must the creator be effaced for the artwork to become an Absolute?"

1. Vinteuil the man is described as timid, sad, and unremarkable. How does the sheer power of the Septet retroactively absolve his pathetic biography?
   - 作者的死亡：凡特伊作为一个懦弱父亲的世俗身份，如何被他天才的音乐作品彻底抹去？

2. Consider Mlle Vinteuil's friend deciphering the "secret hieroglyphs" of the composer's sketches. How does the messy, physical act of composition threaten the illusion of spontaneous divine creation?
   - 草稿的污点：从凌乱的手稿中拼凑出伟大作品，这种“物质性”劳动是否削弱了音乐的神圣感？

3. Nattiez calls this effort to efface the poietic (creative) dimension "Utopian." How does the text struggle between showing the labor of art and treating art as eternal?
   - 乌托邦式的掩盖：普鲁斯特为何极力想让我们忘记这首曲子是被“写”出来的，而更愿称之为被“召唤”出来的？

4. If Vinteuil's music is the redemptive model for Marcel's own book, what does this imply about the structure of the *Recherche* itself?
   - 文学的镜像：凡特伊用七重奏救赎了自己的一生，这是否暗示了《追忆》这部小说也将成为马塞尔唯一的不朽证明？
