# Self-Test — Chapter 004

## A. Recognition Check

Give a first reading value:

1. de
2. à
3. mais
4. comme

## B. Form Check

No forms are tested in this chapter.

## C. Function Check

Name the function:

1. et
2. sans
3. dans

## D. Sentence Check

Mark the function word:

1. La mémoire de la sensation.
2. Une image dans la mémoire.
3. Une sensation comme une image.

## E. English Control Check

Explain why small words cannot be skipped.

## F. Gap Diagnosis

If you recognize nouns but miss relations, review function-word cards.

## Answer Notes

1. `de` = of / from / relation.
2. `à` = to / at / relation.
3. `mais` = but / contrast.
4. `comme` = as / like.
5. `et` adds; `sans` marks absence; `dans` marks location or frame.
