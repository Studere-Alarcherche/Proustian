# Question Gradient

The question gradient keeps public reading pages focused. A question should move from scene to method, then back to Proust.

## Root Question

How can a passage become a reading entrance into Proustian signs, memory, sensation, attention, and writing?

## Starting Questions

- What pressure does this scene place on the reader?
- What sign appears before it is understood?
- What kind of mistake, delay, or misrecognition organizes the scene?
- Which secondary lens clarifies the scene without replacing Proust?
- What form of attention does the passage train?

## U1-U6 Questions

- U1｜Signs / Method: What makes a sign readable before it is understood?
- U2｜Memory / Time / Space: How does involuntary memory reopen time through place and sensation?
- U3｜Music / Ineffable: How does music carry truth before it can be explained?
- U4｜Visuality / Gaze / Photography / Attention: What forms of attention make seeing into reading?
- U5｜Space / Sensory History: How do atmosphere, sound, smell, and place gather collective time?
- U6｜Writing Apparatus: How does the novel turn reading into a method for writing?
