# Translation to Cards Policy

Translation Training may produce cards, but they are not French textbook cards.

Use translation-derived cards for:

- paraphrase patterns
- conceptual transfer
- English expression
- phrase-level rewriting
- recurring errors

Do not mix these cards with French Reading Textbook grammar cards unless the card is explicitly about French reading structure.

Card output should record:

- source exercise
- target skill
- prompt
- answer
- review status
