# LTRP EN-CN Translation Lab Protocol

Translation Lab now lives inside Learn to Read Proust [EN-CN]. It is no longer a separate track and not a GPT translation service.

## What the Lab Preserves

- The reader's translation attempts.
- Mistakes, hesitations, and revisions.
- Judgment notes about loss, gain, rhythm, pressure, and recomposition.
- Links back to passage anchors and movement cards.

## Permitted Source Use

The lab may use:

- User-provided attempts.
- Public-domain material.
- Properly excerpted short passage anchors.
- Local references where copyright status is clearly marked.

The lab should not publish long copyrighted passages or whole translated sections.

## Working Sequence

1. Identify the passage boundary.
2. Record copyright and use status.
3. Preserve the reader's attempt before analysis.
4. Name the reading pressure.
5. Compare close rendering and interpretive rendering only where the reader has work to inspect.
6. Extract a Translation Card, Movement Card, or Training Note after actual work exists.

## What the Lab Must Not Do

- Do not generate a final translation as a service.
- Do not erase failed attempts.
- Do not replace judgment with fluent prose.
- Do not publish raw internal agent notes.
- Do not turn the lab into generic translation theory.

## Output Types

- Translation Card: a bounded note on loss, gain, choice, and evidence.
- Movement Card: a reusable sentence movement extracted from a passage.
- Training Note: a concrete reading or writing action to repeat later.

## Review Gate

No card becomes stable until it has been checked against the passage and the reader's own judgment.
