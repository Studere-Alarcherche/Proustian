## Required Practice Elements

Each chapter must contain:

1. **Primary Work Zone**
   Choose one sentence, passage zone, or anchor. Do not ask the learner to work on the whole passage at once.
2. **Recognition Drill**
   The learner marks something visible: verb, return point, insertion, extension, sensory contact, or completion point.
3. **Recomposition Drill**
   The learner writes a rough Chinese attempt before AI feedback.
4. **Repair Drill**
   The learner repairs one local issue only.
5. **Thinking Question**
   The learner answers one judgment question, not a recall question.
6. **Transfer Drill**
   The learner applies the same move to a new sentence, new passage, or original Chinese sentence.
7. **Movement Card**
   The chapter ends with one lightweight card.

Practice must create friction inside language, not administrative burden outside language.

## 4. Recognition Drill

> [!task] Recognition Drill｜Before AI
> Mark or identify the structure before explanation.
>
> 1. Mark the main clause, inserted phrase, delayed arrival, or point of completion.
> 2. Mark what makes the sentence difficult.
> 3. Write one sentence naming the pressure.

## 5. Recomposition Drill

> [!task] Recomposition Drill｜Rough Chinese First
> Write a rough Chinese rendering before AI feedback.
>
> 1. Do not make it elegant.
> 2. Mark where the Chinese becomes too smooth.
> 3. Mark where meaning completes.

## 6. AI Feedback

> [!tip] AI Feedback Prompt
> I will give you my attempt first.
>
> Do not give a final translation.
> Do not rewrite the whole passage for me.
>
> Evaluate my attempt as a training draft.
>
> Give no more than 3 feedback points, one repair instruction, and one thinking question.

## 7. Repair Drill

> [!example] Repair Drill
> Repair one local problem, or move to the card if the attempt works.

## 8. Thinking Question

> [!question] Thinking Question
> Ask one judgment question that cannot be answered by recall alone.

## 9. Transfer Drill

> [!example] Transfer Drill
> Apply the same movement to a new sentence or a short original Chinese sentence.
