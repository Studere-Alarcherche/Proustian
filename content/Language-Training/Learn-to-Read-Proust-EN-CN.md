# Learn to Read Proust [EN-CN]

Learn to Read Proust [EN-CN] is a passage-based textbook studio inside Proustian. It absorbs the old Translation Lab into a broader reading and prose-training system.

This is not a generic English textbook, not a generic translation workbook, and not a separate product. It works from already split and dismantled *In Search of Lost Time* materials, then turns selected short passage anchors into reading actions: sentence movement, translation judgment, Chinese recomposition, and prose formation.

Translation is used as pressure. It is not outsourced to AI and not treated as an answer machine. The reader's attempts, mistakes, revisions, and judgments remain part of the training record.

## Track Boundary

This is the **EN-CN textbook track**, not the French precision track.

- The French track trains direct attention to French: word recognition, function words, sentence skeletons, and grammar needed to keep the French page visible.
- The EN-CN track trains movement across English and Chinese: English sentence pressure, Chinese recomposition, translation judgment, and prose formation.
- The two tracks meet at Proust, but they should remain visibly separate in the directory and in chapter planning.
