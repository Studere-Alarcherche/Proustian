## Lab Rule

This Lab begins from Recherche, moves through comparative pressure, produces induction, and returns to Recherche.

If the comparative author becomes the center, stop and revise.

## 1. Recherche Anchor

Record a bounded Proust passage anchor.

- source
- volume or section
- passage boundary
- short non-infringing cue
- reading pressure

Do not publish long copyrighted passages.

Source text coordinates:

- Proust English translation link or local edition
- short English Proust excerpt for comparison
- English translation or comparative source link where available
- Chinese translation source status
- copyright or use note

Use comparable English passages rather than merely listing sources. The Lab is a reading apparatus, not a quotation archive.

## 2. Proustian Pressure

Name the reading problem before comparison.

Possible pressure types:

- delayed revelation
- sentence expansion
- suspended recognition
- memory-triggered reclassification
- social perception
- sensory emergence
- temporal unfolding

## 3. Comparative Passage

Introduce the comparative passage only as pressure.

- author
- work
- bounded passage anchor
- contrast function
- why this passage reveals something in Recherche

## 4. Stylistic Matrix

Use [[Stylistic-Matrix|Stylistic Matrix]] fields selectively.

Required fields:

- sentence movement
- temporal logic
- perceptual regime
- rhythm
- return to Recherche

## 5. Induction Tasks

Write tasks that force the reader to infer structure.

Do not give the abstraction first.

Required actions:

- mark movement
- compare rhythm
- trace temporal logic
- identify contrast
- state the emerging pattern

## 6. Structural Restatement

The reader restates the mechanism in their own words.

The restatement should say:

- what Proust is doing
- what the comparative passage made visible
- what difference mattered
- what can now be recognized on return

## 7. Return to Recherche

End by returning to the Proust passage.

Required final question:

- What can the reader now perceive in Recherche that was harder to perceive before comparison?
