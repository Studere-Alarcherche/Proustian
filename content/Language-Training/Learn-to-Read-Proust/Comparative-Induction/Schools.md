# Schools

Schools are not author lists. They are modes of organizing reality through language.

Each School exists only if it can sharpen the reader's perception of Recherche. A School that becomes a literary-history category has drifted.

## School Record Template

Each School should be recorded with:

- School name
- central mode of organizing reality
- Proustian pressure it reveals
- comparative pressure it introduces
- reading actions it trains
- likely passage anchors
- return-to-Proust question

## Initial Schools

These are provisional operating Schools for Phase 1 and early Phase 2.

## Delayed Revelation

Reality becomes readable only after temporal unfolding, retrospective recognition, and belated structural connection.

Proustian pressure:

- delayed revelation
- retrospective clarification
- sentence expansion
- memory-triggered reorganization

Comparative use:

- contrast with more immediate, wave-like, ruptured, historical, or impersonal modes.

Return question:

- What does Recherche make perceptible only after delay?

## Wave Consciousness

Consciousness appears as rhythmic wave, shifting interior light, and mobile perceptual surface.

Proustian pressure it clarifies:

- Proustian consciousness does not merely flow; it often unfolds through delayed recognition.

Phase 2 anchor:

- Proust and Woolf.

Return question:

- Where does Proust differ from wave-like continuity by withholding recognition?

## Temporal Rupture

Time appears through fracture, recursion, simultaneity, and broken sequence.

Proustian pressure it clarifies:

- Recherche disturbs chronology, but its disturbances often move toward unfolding, recovery, and reconfiguration.

Phase 2 anchor:

- Proust and Faulkner.

Return question:

- How does Proust make time unstable without making rupture the dominant form?

## Historical Reflection

Narrative intelligence organizes perception through historical distance, reflective authority, and retrospective judgment.

Proustian pressure it clarifies:

- Recherche thinks historically, but its recognition often emerges from sensory, social, and affective pressure before becoming reflective structure.

Phase 2 anchor:

- Proust and Yourcenar.

Return question:

- What happens when historical reflection is compared with Proustian sensory emergence?

## Impersonal Precision

Prose disciplines perception through restraint, selection, and impersonal exactness.

Proustian pressure it clarifies:

- Proustian expansion is not looseness; it is an alternate precision of relation, qualification, and deferred recognition.

Possible later anchor:

- Proust and Flaubert.

Return question:

- What kind of precision does Proust create through expansion?
