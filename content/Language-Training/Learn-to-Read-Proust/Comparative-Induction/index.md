# Proustian Comparative Induction

Proustian Comparative Induction is a multilingual high-induction reading apparatus inside Learn to Read Proust.

It is not a comparative literature course. It is not a survey of literary history. It is not a translation-comparison project. It exists to form a high-order reader through *In Search of Lost Time*.

Proust remains the gravitational center. Comparative texts, languages, translations, and literary traditions serve one function: they sharpen the reader's perception of Recherche.

For the English comparative Labs, the primary Proust anchor is the English translation as a literary text inside the English reading field. The French source may remain available for source-language calibration, but it is not the main comparative object in these Labs.

## Public / Internal Boundary

This track is internal working material unless a page is explicitly reviewed and marked public.

Raw prompts, agent constitutions, lab templates, Chinese genealogy working notes, and draft comparative chapters must not be treated as public reader-facing material. If a Comparative Induction page becomes public later, it must be English, restrained, and centered on what the comparison returns to *Recherche*.

## Boundary

Comparative Induction belongs to Language Training, but language training here does not mean memorized grammar, collected vocabulary, conversational fluency, or exam exercises.

Language training means the formation of perception:

- stylistic recognition
- sentence-pressure perception
- temporal logic detection
- rhythm awareness
- comparative induction
- multilingual reading transfer
- literary pattern recognition
- perception through variation

## 中文文学谱系比较

中文谱系比较现在单独放在目录：[[Chinese-Literary-Genealogy/index|中文文学谱系比较]]。

这个目录不把任何译者作为章节命名中心，也不把某一个中文译本写成唯一基准。中文译本坐标的作用，是观察 Proust 在现代汉语中如何成为一个有文体主体性的“翻译文学文本”。

这里要处理的问题不是“中国文学和普鲁斯特找相似点”，而是：Proust 的中文译本进入中国阅读场之后，怎样影响中国读者对长句、意识流、记忆写作、感官细节、高级散文、翻译腔、现代文学腔调的想象。

它属于 Learn to Read Proust / From Proust to World Literature 下面的“比较文学—谱系学”附属增强章节，不进入 Proustian 六单元主干。

中文谱系比较的任务是把 Proust 的阅读压力转译到中文文学经验和写作训练中：白先勇、钱锺书、张爱玲、《红楼梦》、沈从文、汪曾祺、鲁迅等，只能在具体文体机制上进入，不能作为泛泛的影响史名单。

## Source Text Policy

Labs may attach source text coordinates, but they should not become quotation archives.

- Use public-domain source links when available.
- In English-comparative Labs, use Proust's English translation as the primary passage text.
- In Chinese genealogy pages, treat Chinese translations of Proust as translation-literature objects inside the Chinese reading field; do not hard-code one translator as the center.
- Use short comparable excerpts rather than long transcriptions.
- Keep copyrighted modern works as passage fields, bibliographic references, or reader-supplied excerpts.
- Do not attach full Chinese translations unless the passage is supplied for private study or is clearly cleared for publication.
- Treat Chinese translations of Proust as mediated literary objects, not as transparent replacements for the French source.

## Method

The system operates through strong induction:

- repeated exposure
- comparison through similarity and difference
- pattern emergence
- gradual structural recognition
- delayed abstraction

Do not frontload abstract theory. Do not reduce passages to themes or summaries. Do not allow comparative authors to become equal centers. Every comparison must eventually return to Recherche.
