# LTRP EN-CN Textbook Studio

This studio organizes selected passage anchors into a textbook sequence for reading, recomposition, translation judgment, and prose training.

It does not publish raw internal agent files. It does not ask AI to translate whole passages. It does not turn Proust into a generic language-learning object.

## Studio Function

- Hold the eight-part textbook architecture.
- Convert selected passage anchors into chapter work.
- Preserve the reader's EN-CN attempts, revisions, and judgments.
- Extract Translation Cards, Movement Cards, and Training Notes from actual work.
- Keep the public surface Markdown-first and readable in Quartz.

## Workflow

1. Choose a short passage anchor with a verified boundary.
2. Record source, edition, local location, start words, and end words.
3. Identify the sentence movement or reading pressure.
4. Study how English delays, inserts, qualifies, suspends, or redirects thought.
5. Ask what Chinese must recompose rather than merely translate.
6. Add the reader's translation attempts only when they exist.
7. Extract one reusable movement or writing move.
8. Return the chapter to Proust rather than to generic translation theory.

## Studio Outputs

- Chapter draft
- Translation Card
- Movement Card
- Training Note
- Writing Exercise
- Review question for later digestion

## Guardrails

- Use short, properly bounded passage anchors.
- Do not publish long copyrighted passages.
- Do not decide the final intellectual thesis.
- Do not replace French precision work already active in [[index|Learn to Read Proust]].
- Do not webify the studio before the Markdown structure proves its value.
