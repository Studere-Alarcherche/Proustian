# Language Training

This section contains the language precision layer of Proustian.

French is not a separate project and not a seventh course unit. It serves Proustian reading: original passages, Deleuze's terminology, French theory syntax, French Anchors, Pattern Cards, Passage Cards, and passage-level precision.

The current focus is reading only.

No conversation training.  
No tourist French.  
No general grammar course.  
No performance-oriented language learning.

## Learn to Read French for Proust — Book I

Book I starts from A0 and builds a French reading machine.

Its goal is not to make the reader fluent in French immediately. Its goal is to train recognition, sentence patience, word-family awareness, function-word logic, tense awareness, and slow passage-level reading.
