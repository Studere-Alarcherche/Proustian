# Comparative Literary Induction

This textbook treats Proust in English translation as part of the English literary world.

It uses Norton-style literary pedagogy: close reading, comparison drills, critical questions, writing prompts, and concept cards.

The goal is not to compare names. The goal is to train judgment through Proustian reading, English literary counterpoints, and human-first criticism.

Each unit begins with Proust, uses a comparative author as pressure, and returns to Proust with sharper perception.
