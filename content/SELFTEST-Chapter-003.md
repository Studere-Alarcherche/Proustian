# Self-Test — Chapter 003

## A. Recognition Check

Match pattern to word:

1. `-tion`
2. `-ité`
3. `-ment`
4. circumflex

Words: attention, sensibilité, mouvement, forêt.

## B. Form Check

What is the difference between `actif` and `active`?

## C. Function Check

No function words are tested in this chapter.

## D. Sentence Check

Mark the pattern:

1. Une sensation visible.
2. Le mouvement de la mémoire.

## E. English Control Check

Why do word families matter more than isolated memorization?

## F. Gap Diagnosis

If you know individual words but miss endings, review suffix cards.

## Answer Notes

1. `attention` = `-tion`.
2. `sensibilité` = `-ité`.
3. `mouvement` = `-ment`.
4. `forêt` = circumflex / possible lost `s`.
5. `actif` is masculine; `active` is feminine.
