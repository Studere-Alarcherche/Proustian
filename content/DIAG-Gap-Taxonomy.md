# Gap Taxonomy

## Sentence Visibility

The reader cannot identify the full sentence.

## Subject / Verb

The reader cannot locate the subject group or conjugated verb.

## Forms

The reader does not recognize tense, person, number, gender, or article.

## Function Words

The reader misses a structural word such as `de`, `à`, `que`, or `qui`.

## Word Order

The reader cannot track basic order or delayed material.

## English Control

The reader cannot produce a plain English structural check.

## Proustian Entry

The reader over-interprets before parsing.
