# SKILL-Proustian-Alignment

## Purpose

Ensure language training remains aligned with Proustian's core reading project.

This skill keeps the French Reading Textbook, Translation Training, cards, and diagnostics from drifting into generic language study.

## When to Use

- every chapter review
- vocabulary selection
- card design
- translation training
- final examination
- after adding any new language-training file

## Inputs

- target material
- intended Proustian unit connection
- reading pressure
- sentence, scene, object, sound, image, or passage for textual return
- risk assessment for generic French or over-Proustianization

## Procedure

1. Identify which Proustian question the material serves.
2. Identify the reading pressure: memory, sign, time, space, music, visuality, sensation, social form, writing apparatus.
3. Check whether the material is too generic.
4. Check whether Proust is forced too early.
5. Require textual return: sentence, scene, object, sound, image, passage.

Core questions:

U1 — Signs / Method  
How does Proust train the reader to read signs rather than merely follow plot, psychology, or memory?

U2 — Memory / Time / Space  
How does Proust transform memory from recollection into a structure of signs, time, space, and artistic discovery?

U3 — Music / Ineffable  
How does Vinteuil's music move from emotional sign to artistic truth, ineffable experience, and novelistic structure?

U4 — Visuality / Gaze  
How does Proust transform seeing into a discipline of attention, misrecognition, capture, and artistic truth?

U5 — Sensory History / Social Space  
How do spaces and sensory environments in Proust produce social time and historical subjectivity?

U6 — Writing Apparatus  
How can reading, passage cards, language training, and conceptual transfer become teachable, writable, reader-facing forms?

## Output Format

## Proustian Alignment

### Unit Connection

### Reading Pressure

### Textual Return

### Risk of Generic French

### Risk of Over-Proustianization

### Verdict

Pass / Revise / Reject

## Audit Questions

- Which Proustian question does the material serve?
- What reading pressure does it train?
- Is the material too generic?
- Is Proust being forced too early?
- What is the textual return?
- Does the material strengthen source-text access, not just thematic talk?

## Failure Signals

- generic French with no target pressure
- decorative Proust reference
- too advanced too early
- no connection to six core questions
- no textual return
- language work does not support reading pressure
