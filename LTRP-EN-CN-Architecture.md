# LTRP EN-CN Architecture

## Architecture Summary

Learn to Read Proust [EN-CN] uses an 8 Part x 6 Chapter architecture.

Each chapter should define:

- one reading move
- passage criteria
- one English reading or style skill
- one Chinese recomposition skill
- one writing formation skill

The architecture is flat, packet-based, and open. Do not turn Parts, Chapters, or secondary concepts into a folder hierarchy.

## Passage Criteria Checklist

Use this checklist before selecting a passage:

- The passage comes from a real, traceable source.
- The passage trains the chapter move.
- The passage is short enough for close work.
- The passage has enough syntactic or perceptual pressure to justify use.
- The passage can support English reading, Chinese recomposition, and writing transfer.
- The passage does not require FR-EN-CN collation in the current phase.
