MUSIC, THE INEFFABLE, AND NOVELISTIC STRUCTURE

Unit 3 turns Proustian memory towards the ultimate sign system: Music. How does Vinteuil's little phrase evolve from a mere anchor of romantic nostalgia (Swann) into the ineffable architecture of the Septet (The Captive), ultimately serving as the redemptive model for literature itself?
