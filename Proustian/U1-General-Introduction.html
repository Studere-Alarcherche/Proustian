METHOD, RHIZOME, AND APPRENTICESHIP

A decentralized, roaming digital paper. U1 is the methodological entrance. It dismantles the cliché that Proust is solely about "memory," replacing it with an active apprenticeship in decoding signs, cognitive training, and forced thought. Scan the 10 micro-packs below. Click the questions that generate heat. Use the scene anchors and bilingual seeds to establish your Kite-Line Reading.
