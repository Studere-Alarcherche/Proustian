Chapter 004 — Function Words I

Before You Begin

The first three chapters trained you to notice large recognizable words. This chapter turns to small words.

Small words are easy to skip because they look thin. But in French reading, they often tell you how the sentence is built.

Small words build relations.

What You Will Learn

You will learn to:

  • recognize nine high-frequency function words;
  • ask what each small word connects;
  • avoid automatic translations such as “de = of” and “à = to”;
  • read relation before interpretation;
  • compress short phrases into relation notes.

Core Reading Move

Small words build relations.

The action is:

  1. Circle the function word.
  2. Identify what it connects.
  3. Name the relation.
  4. Delay polished translation.

1. Explanation

Function words do not usually carry the biggest dictionary meaning. They organize relations.

The basic question is not “What is the English word?” The basic question is “What relation does this create?”

2. Recognition

de

de can mark source, possession, relation, or material.

  • la mémoire de l'enfance → relation / possession: memory of childhood
  • une impression de silence → relation / quality: an impression of silence
  • une table de bois → material: a wooden table
  • sortir de la chambre → source: come out of the room

Do not read every de as a flat “of.”

à

à can mark direction, destination, relation, or use.

  • aller à la chambre → direction / destination
  • un livre à lire → use / task: a book to read
  • une attention à l'image → relation / target
  • donner à la mémoire → direction toward a receiver

Do not read every à as a flat “to.”

et / mais

et adds. mais contrasts.

  • la mémoire et l'image → addition
  • la mémoire revient, mais l'image reste obscure → contrast

dans / sur

dans places something inside a frame. sur places something on, about, or over a surface or topic.

  • dans la chambre → inside a room
  • dans la mémoire → within memory
  • sur la table → on the table
  • sur la mémoire → about memory

avec / sans

avec marks accompaniment. sans marks absence.

  • avec une sensation → with a sensation
  • sans image → without an image

comme

comme can mean as or like. It creates comparison or role.

  • comme une image → like an image
  • comme lecteur → as a reader

4. Pattern Work

PATTERN-de

Pattern

de marks source, possession, relation, or material.

Reading Move

Ask what two elements are being linked and what kind of relation holds them.

What It Usually Does

It attaches one noun or phrase to another.

Do Not

Do not translate it automatically as “of.”

Mini Example

une impression de silence → an impression related to silence.

Where It Returns

Every chapter after this one, especially Proustian noun groups.

PATTERN-a

Pattern

à marks direction, destination, relation, or use.

Reading Move

Ask whether something points toward a place, task, receiver, or target.

What It Usually Does

It creates orientation.

Do Not

Do not translate it automatically as “to.”

Mini Example

un livre à lire → a book for reading / to be read.

Where It Returns

Infinitive phrases, reading tasks, and later sentence attachments.

PATTERN-dans

Pattern

dans places something inside or within a frame.

Reading Move

Find the frame.

What It Usually Does

It can frame physical space or mental space.

Do Not

Do not assume the frame is always physical.

Mini Example

dans la mémoire → within memory.

Where It Returns

Proustian rooms, memory spaces, and mental frames.

PATTERN-avec

Pattern

avec marks accompaniment.

Reading Move

Ask what is present together with the main action or thing.

What It Usually Does

It adds an accompanying element: person, tool, quality, or manner.

Do Not

Do not ignore it as a small optional word.

Mini Example

avec attention → with attention / attentively.

Where It Returns

Descriptions of reading, perception, and attention.

5. Mini Reading

Dans la chambre, le lecteur regarde une image avec attention. La mémoire de l'enfance revient, mais elle revient sans clarté. Sur la table, un livre sur le temps reste ouvert, comme une invitation à lire.

First pass: circle every function word.

Second pass: write what each one connects.

Third pass: compress the relations before translating.

6. Reading Notes

Proustian reading depends on relations: memory of childhood, sensation in a room, attention to an image, a book about time, a perception without clarity. If you skip the small words, you keep the vocabulary but lose the structure.

This chapter does not teach every use of de or à. It teaches a reading habit: relation first.

For each phrase, write the two connected elements.

  1. la mémoire de l'enfance
  2. une sensation dans la chambre
  3. une image sur la table
  4. un regard avec attention
  5. une impression sans forme
  6. la lecture et la mémoire
  7. un livre sur Proust
  8. comme une image
  9. une invitation à lire
  10. obscur mais visible

Exercise Set 5 — Compress before translating

Write a relation note, not a full translation.

  1. dans la chambre
  2. sur la mémoire
  3. avec une sensation obscure
  4. sans image visible
  5. comme un souvenir
  6. à la fenêtre
  7. de la matière
  8. et le silence
  9. mais la perception change
  10. un livre à relire
Show answer

1 inside room frame; 2 about memory; 3 accompanied by an obscure sensation; 4 absence of visible image; 5 comparison to a memory; 6 direction/destination toward the window; 7 source/material/relation depending sentence; 8 addition of silence; 9 contrast with changing perception; 10 book for rereading.

Review Task — Relation map

From the mini reading, list five function words. For each, write:

  • function word;
  • what it connects;
  • relation created;
  • one bad mechanical translation to avoid.

Check Your Answer

You are succeeding if you can explain the relation before translating. A small word is not a decoration. It is a hinge.

What You Can Now Do

You can slow down at de, à, dans, sur, avec, sans, comme, et, and mais. You can ask what they connect and what relation they build.
