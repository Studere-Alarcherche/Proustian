Palette

The system is dark, warm, and literary. Quiet gold is an accent for thresholds, rules, and active links, never a bright yellow button language.

Typography

Serif type carries the reading surface. Sans-serif is reserved for labels and operational clarity. Monospace appears only as textual apparatus, not fake telemetry.

Anti-pattern Warnings

These patterns should be rejected during homepage implementation, even if they look polished in isolation.

Avoid SaaS landing page rhythm

No conversion funnel, pricing-page energy, or stacked marketing calls to action.

Avoid Generic card grid

Entrance panels must carry reading functions, not interchangeable product features.

Avoid Cheap bright yellow

Gold should appear as quiet illumination, not warning paint or startup branding.
